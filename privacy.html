Privacy Policy for Squeeze

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Squeeze has no accounts and no sign-in, and we do not collect your name, your email address or anything else that identifies you. Your progress stays on your device. The one thing that does leave it is an advertising identifier, which the ads in the game need — this page explains exactly what that is and how to switch it off.

What the game stores, and where

Squeeze saves your progress on your own device only: best scores, coins, skins you own, Adventure levels, awards and your settings. It is kept in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app removes it.

The game also keeps a count of which hours of the day you tend to play, so that its reminders arrive at a sensible time rather than at random. That count is a list of twenty-four numbers. It never leaves your device, it is not sent to us, and turning reminders off in Settings stops it being used.

If you have Android's backup or Apple's iCloud backup turned on, your device may include this save file in its own backup. That is handled by Google or Apple under their own policies, not by us, and we cannot read it.

Advertising

Squeeze shows ads through Google AdMob. There are three kinds: a banner on the main menu and in the shop, an occasional full-screen ad between runs, and videos you choose to watch in exchange for something in the game. Only the last kind is ever your decision, and nothing in the game is locked behind watching one.

To do this, AdMob uses your device's advertising identifier — a resettable number your phone gives to apps, which is not your name and is not tied to your Google or Apple account. AdMob may also receive your approximate location (from your IP address), your device model and your operating system version. It uses these to choose an ad and to measure whether it worked. We never see any of it.

Google's use of this data is covered by the Google Business Data Responsibility page and the How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services page.

Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks your permission before any ad loads, using Google's own consent form. If you say no to personalised ads, you still see ads, but they are chosen without the identifier.
  • On iOS, the game asks separately through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. Declining changes nothing about how the game plays.
  • On any device, you can reset or delete the advertising identifier entirely: on Android under Settings → Privacy → Ads, and on iOS under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • You can remove the ads. The one-off "Remove ads" purchase in the shop stops the banner and the full-screen ads for good. The reward videos stay available, because they are ones you choose.

Purchases

Coin packs and the one-off ad removal are sold through Google Play and the Apple App Store. When you buy something, the store handles the payment and tells the app only whether the purchase succeeded. We never see your card details, your billing address or your store account.

Google's handling of that transaction is covered by the Google Privacy Policy, and Apple's by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Notifications

Squeeze sends a few reminders a day about your own progress — the level you stopped on, your best score, a gift waiting in the shop. They are written on your phone from your own save file. Nothing about you is sent anywhere to produce them, and there is no server involved.

You can turn them off under Settings → Reminders, or in your phone's own notification settings for the app.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect names, email addresses or phone numbers.
  • We do not ask for your precise location, and the app has no location permission.
  • We do not use analytics. Nothing reports to us about how you play.
  • We do not sell your data to anyone.
  • We do not knowingly direct the game at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them. Ad content is limited to the "General audiences" rating.

Permissions

The app asks for network access, which billing and ads both need, and for permission to send notifications, which you can decline or revoke at any time. It does not ask for the camera, the microphone, contacts, files or location.

Children

Squeeze is suitable for all ages, and ad content is restricted accordingly, but the game is not aimed at children and is not part of a families programme. Purchases are controlled by the store account on the device; parents can require a password for every purchase in the Google Play or App Store settings.

Your rights

Because we hold nothing about you, there is nothing for us to show you or delete. Progress lives on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app. For the advertising identifier, the controls listed above are the ones that apply, and they are yours rather than ours. If you would like anything about this confirmed in writing, use the contact address below.
