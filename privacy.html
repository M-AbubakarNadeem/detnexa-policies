Privacy Policy

For Squeeze, published by Detnexa Technologies. Last updated 26 September 2026.

The short version

Squeeze has no accounts, no sign-in and no server of ours behind it. We never learn your name or your email address. Your save file sits on your phone and nowhere else.

One thing does leave your device, and that is the advertising identifier the ads need. You can reset it at any time, or buy the one-off ad removal and be done with it. Everything below is the detail.

What the game keeps, and where

Your progress is stored on your own phone, in the app's private storage. Best scores, coins, the skins you own, which Adventure levels you have finished, your awards, your settings. Uninstall the game and all of it goes with it.

There is one other thing in that file worth mentioning. Squeeze counts which hours of the day you tend to play, so that its reminders turn up when you are actually likely to be playing rather than at three in the morning. It is twenty-four numbers and nothing else. It never leaves the phone, we never see it, and turning reminders off in Settings stops it being used at all.

If you have Android Backup or Apple's iCloud Backup switched on, your phone may copy that save file into its own backup. That is Google's process or Apple's, running under their policies rather than ours, and we cannot read what ends up there.

Advertising

Squeeze is free and pays for itself with ads, served through Google AdMob. There are three kinds: a banner on the main menu and in the shop, a full screen ad between runs now and then, and videos you can choose to watch in exchange for something in the game. Only that last kind is ever your decision, and nothing in Squeeze is locked behind watching one.

To choose an ad, AdMob uses your device's advertising identifier. That is a resettable number your phone hands to apps. It is not your name and it is not tied to your Google or Apple account. AdMob may also work out roughly where you are from your IP address, and read your device model and operating system version. It uses those to pick an ad and to measure whether the ad worked. None of it reaches us.

What Google does with that data is covered by the Google Business Data Responsibility page and by How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland Squeeze asks your permission before any ad loads, using Google's own consent form. Say no to personalised ads and you still see ads. They are just chosen without the identifier.
  • On iOS Apple asks separately, through its App Tracking Transparency prompt. Declining changes nothing about how the game plays.
  • On any phone You can reset or delete the advertising identifier outright. On Android it is under Settings, then Privacy, then Ads. On iOS it is under Settings, then Privacy and Security, then Tracking.
  • Or remove the ads entirely The one-off purchase in the shop stops the banner and the full screen ads for good. Reward videos stay available, because those are ones you choose to watch.

Purchases

Coin packs and the ad removal are sold through Google Play and the Apple App Store. The store takes the payment and tells the game one thing only: whether it went through. Your card number, your billing address and your store account never come near us.

Google's handling of that transaction is covered by the Google Privacy Policy, and Apple's by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Reminders

Squeeze sends a few notifications a day, and every one of them is about your own progress. The level you stopped on. A score worth beating. A gift sitting unclaimed in the shop. They are written on your phone, from your own save file. Nothing about you is sent anywhere to produce them, and there is no server involved at any point.

Turn them off under Settings, then Reminders, or in your phone's own notification settings for the app.

What Squeeze does not do

  • No analytics, anywhere in the game. Nothing reports back to us about how you play, how long you played, or how far you got. We genuinely do not know.
  • No names, email addresses or phone numbers. There is nothing to type them into.
  • No precise location. The app holds no location permission at all.
  • Nothing is sold on. Not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to anyone.
  • Not aimed at children under 13, and ad content is capped at the General audiences rating.

Permissions

Network access, which the ads and the purchases both need. Permission to send notifications, which you can refuse outright or revoke later. That is the whole list. No camera, no microphone, no contacts, no files, no location.

Children

Squeeze is suitable for all ages and its ads are rated accordingly, but the game is not built for children and it is not part of Google's Families programme. Purchases run through whichever store account is signed in on the device. If you want every purchase to require a password, both Google Play and the App Store let you set that, and it is worth doing on a phone a child uses.

Your rights

We hold nothing about you, so there is nothing for us to show you and nothing for us to delete. Your progress lives on your phone and goes when the app does. For the advertising identifier, the controls listed above are the ones that apply, and they belong to you rather than to us. If you would like any of that confirmed in writing, the address is below.
